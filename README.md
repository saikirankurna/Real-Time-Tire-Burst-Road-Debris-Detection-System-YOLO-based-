# Real-Time-Tire-Burst-Road-Debris-Detection-System-YOLO-based-
This system is designed to enhance vehicle safety by detecting tire bursts and road debris in real time using advanced computer vision techniques. Leveraging the YOLO (You Only Look Once) object detection framework, the system can quickly identify hazardous situations on the road, allowing timely alerts and preventive action.
Key Features:
1. Real-Time Detection: Continuously monitors the road and vehicle tires through live video feed.
2. Tire Burst Identification: Detects tire blowouts or abnormal tire conditions to prevent accidents.
3. Road Debris Detection: Identifies objects or debris on the road that could pose safety hazards.
4. High Accuracy & Speed: YOLO-based deep learning ensures fast and precise detection.
5. Alerts & Notifications: Can be integrated with vehicle dashboards or alert systems to notify the driver immediately.
6. Scalable & Extensible: Can be deployed on different vehicles or integrated with smart traffic systems.

Technical Stack:
1. Framework: YOLOv8 / YOLOv5 (for object detection)
2. Libraries: OpenCV (image processing), NumPy, PyTorch
3. Input: Real-time video feed from camera mounted on vehicle
4. Output: Bounding boxes and alerts for detected tire bursts or road debris

Applications:
1. Automotive safety systems
2. Fleet management and monitoring
3. Smart city traffic safety initiatives
